# Apex MediLab Policy: Patient Privacy, HIPAA & Confidentiality Standards
Policy Reference: AML-POL-PRIV-015 | Version: 3.1 | Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Statutory Compliance
Apex MediLab strictly complies with HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act), DISHA, and international data protection standards regarding Protected Health Information (PHI).

## 2. Inbound Caller Verification for Diagnostic Reports
- When any individual calls asking for test results or medical records:
  - Caller ID must be matched against registered patient profile.
  - Secondary verification must be completed by confirming either Date of Birth (DOB) or Registered Order ID.
  - Test results will NEVER be disclosed to an unverified third party without prior written or digital OTP authorization from the patient.
- Sensitive Investigation Results (e.g. HIV I/II, Beta-hCG, Genetic testing, Biopsy malignancy reports) CANNOT be read out in detail over telephone. Patients are respectfully directed to an in-person clinical counseling session or authorized secure patient portal download.

## 3. Data Encryption & Storage
- All electronic health records (EHR) and biometric laboratory parameters are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3.
- Audio recordings of AI voice agent telephone conversations are retained for 90 days for quality assurance and training, subject to caller opt-out.
