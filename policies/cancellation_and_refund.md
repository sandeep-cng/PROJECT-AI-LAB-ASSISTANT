# Apex MediLab Policy: Appointment Cancellation, Rescheduling & Refund Policy
Policy Reference: AML-POL-BILL-007 | Version: 2.1 | Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope
Applies to all outpatient clinic appointments, home sample collections, diagnostic radiology scans, and preventive health checkup packages.

## 2. Cancellation Window & Refund Entitlement
- **Cancellations Made > 2 Hours Prior to Scheduled Slot**:
  - Full **100% refund** credited to original payment source or patient wallet.
  - Zero cancellation fee charged.
- **Cancellations Made < 2 Hours Prior to Scheduled Slot (Phlebotomist Dispatched)**:
  - If a home collection technician has already departed from the central hub, a nominal transit fee of **$5.00 (or ₹150 INR)** is retained; remaining test fee is 100% refunded.
- **Failed Sample Draw / Difficult Vein Access**:
  - If the phlebotomist is unable to successfully draw blood due to vein collapse or patient discomfort, patient may opt for a senior phlebotomist visit free of cost or receive an immediate 100% unconditional refund.

## 3. Rescheduling Terms
- Patients may reschedule any test or home collection slot **up to 1 hour prior** to the appointment without any surcharge.
- Rescheduling can be executed seamlessly via the automated AI voice agent or online patient portal.

## 4. Refund Processing Timelines
- **UPI / Debit Cards / Instant Banking**: 24 to 48 hours.
- **Credit Cards & Net Banking**: 3 to 5 business days depending on the issuing bank.
- **SMS & Email Confirmation**: Digital refund reference receipt dispatched instantly upon cancellation confirmation.
