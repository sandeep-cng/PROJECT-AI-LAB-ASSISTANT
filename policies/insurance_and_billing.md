# Apex MediLab Policy: Insurance, TPA Cashless & Billing Policy
Policy Reference: AML-POL-FIN-009 | Version: 2.4 | Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Billing Transparency
- All diagnostic charges are strictly fixed according to the published Laboratory Test Master.
- Zero hidden surcharges for consumables, sterile needles, or tube barcoding.

## 2. Insurance & Cashless TPA Claims
- Apex MediLab is empaneled with major Third Party Administrators (TPAs) and private medical insurers.
- For cashless outpatient investigation claims:
  - Patient must present valid government photo ID, health insurance policy card, and signed prescription from a registered medical practitioner (MBBS/MD).
  - Pre-authorization approvals take 30 to 60 minutes.
- Reimbursement Billing: Itemized tax invoices containing doctor registration number, official diagnostic codes (CPT / LOINC), and laboratory seal are issued automatically in PDF format upon completion of payment.

## 3. Payment Modes Accepted
- Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, Net Banking, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for home visits.
- Digital payment links can be sent directly via SMS/WhatsApp by the AI voice agent.
