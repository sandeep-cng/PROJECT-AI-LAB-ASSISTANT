# Apex MediLab Policy: Home Sample Collection Standard Operating Procedure
Policy Reference: AML-SOP-OPS-012 | Version: 3.0 | Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Objectives
Ensure uncompromising sterile technique, cold-chain sample integrity, patient safety, and exact appointment punctuality for doorstep pathology services.

## 2. Punctuality & Slot Commitment
- Home visits operate in 60-minute service windows (e.g. 06:30 AM - 07:30 AM, 07:30 AM - 08:30 AM).
- Phlebotomist calls patient 15 minutes prior to arrival.
- If arrival is delayed beyond 20 minutes of the promised window due to traffic or weather, the home delivery convenience charge ($10.00 / ₹200) is automatically waived as a courtesy.

## 3. Identification & Safety Protocol
- All visiting phlebotomists must display official digital Apex MediLab badge with photo ID, employee code, and verified vaccination certificate.
- Mandatory use of brand new, factory-sealed vacutainer needles opened directly in front of the patient.
- Phlebotomists wear fresh nitrile medical gloves, sanitizing hands before and after collection.

## 4. Cold Chain & Specimen Preservation
- Samples immediately transferred to calibrated insulated transport bags equipped with ice-gel packs maintaining internal temperature of **2°C to 8°C**.
- Digital temperature loggers accompany high-sensitivity samples (e.g. coagulations, hormones, molecular genetics).
- Specimens delivered to the regional accessioning laboratory within a maximum transport threshold of **120 minutes**.
