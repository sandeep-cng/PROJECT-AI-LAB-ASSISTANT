# Apex MediLab Policy: Pre-Analytical & Sample Collection Guidelines
Policy Reference: AML-SOP-PAC-001 | Version: 3.2 | Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy defines the standard operating requirements for patient preparation, fasting guidelines, and specimen collection for all blood, urine, and specialized diagnostic pathology examinations.

## 2. Fasting Guidelines by Test Type
- **Comprehensive Lipid Profile (Cholesterol, Triglycerides, HDL, LDL, VLDL)**:
  - Strict fasting duration: **10 to 12 hours**.
  - Plain water consumption is strongly permitted and encouraged to maintain proper hydration.
  - Alcoholic beverages and smoking must be avoided for at least 24 hours prior to sampling.
- **Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS)**:
  - Fasting duration: **8 to 10 hours** overnight.
  - No morning tea, coffee, juice, or chewable mints.
- **Post-Prandial Blood Sugar (PPBS)**:
  - Sample drawn exactly **2 hours after the start of a meal** (not after the meal ends).
- **HbA1c (Glycated Hemoglobin)**:
  - Fasting is **NOT required**. Can be taken any time of day, before or after food.
- **Thyroid Profile (Total T3, Total T4, Ultrasensitive TSH)**:
  - Fasting is **optional / not strictly required**.
  - Blood should preferably be drawn in the early morning (between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM) due to diurnal variation in TSH.
  - If taking Levothyroxine (e.g. Synthroid, Eltroxin), the medication should be taken *after* blood collection unless specifically instructed otherwise by the physician.
- **Liver Function Test (LFT) & Kidney/Renal Function Test (KFT/RFT)**:
  - Overnight fasting of **8 to 10 hours** is recommended for optimal baseline serum indices.
- **Vitamin D (25-OH) & Vitamin B12**:
  - Fasting not required, though morning sampling is preferred.
  - Patients on mega-dose Biotin supplements (>5 mg/day) must discontinue at least 48 hours prior to test to prevent immunoassay interference.

## 3. Urine Specimen Collection Protocol
- **Routine & Microscopic Examination**:
  - Clean-catch, mid-stream first morning urine specimen is preferred.
  - Initial stream discarded into toilet; mid-stream collected in sterile container provided by phlebotomist.
  - Specimen must reach the laboratory within 2 hours of voiding or be refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C.

## 4. Special Patient Considerations
- **Diabetic Patients**: Morning insulin or oral hypoglycemic agents should be deferred until after the fasting sample has been drawn, followed immediately by breakfast.
- **Pediatric & Geriatric Sampling**: Gentle micro-sampling techniques and butterfly needles (23G/25G) are mandatory. Minimum collection volumes must strictly match vacuum tube fill lines.
